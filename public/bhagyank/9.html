Bhagyank 9 Meaning - The Warrior

Ruling planet: Mars (Mangal) · भाग्यांक 9 - योद्धा

Bhagyank 9 is ruled by Mars and blesses you with courage, energy and a humanitarian heart. Your destiny is one of action, protection and service. You are brave, determined and fight for what is right.

People with destiny number 9 carry the energy of Mars. The core themes of this number are courage, energy, compassion. Here is how that tends to show up in daily life.

Best careers for Bhagyank 9

Defence, sports, surgery, engineering, social work and leadership roles.

These fields reward the natural qualities of number 9. Your Mulank (birth number) and Namank (name number) can add other strengths, so read them together with your Bhagyank before making a big career decision.

Examples: dates of birth with Bhagyank 9

Your Bhagyank is 9 when all the digits of your full date of birth reduce to 9. A few examples:

  • 30 November 1975: 3 + 0 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 9 + 7 + 5 = 27 → 2 + 7 = 9 → Bhagyank 9
  • 20 July 1989: 2 + 0 + 7 + 1 + 9 + 8 + 9 = 36 → 3 + 6 = 9 → Bhagyank 9
  • 7 September 2000: 7 + 9 + 2 + 0 + 0 + 0 = 18 → 1 + 8 = 9 → Bhagyank 9

Bhagyank 9: frequently asked questions

What is the ruling planet of Bhagyank 9?+

Bhagyank 9 is ruled by Mars (Mangal) in Vedic numerology. Its energy shapes the life path of people whose full date of birth adds up to 9.

What are the lucky colors and days for Bhagyank 9?+

The lucky colors for Bhagyank 9 are Red, Crimson, Pink, and the favourable days are Tuesday and Thursday.

Which careers suit Bhagyank 9?+

Careers that traditionally suit Bhagyank 9 include: Defence, sports, surgery, engineering, social work and leadership roles.

Which gemstone is linked with Bhagyank 9?+

In traditional numerology, Red Coral (मूंगा) is associated with number 9 and its planet Mars. Please consult a qualified astrologer before wearing any gemstone.

How do I know if my Bhagyank is 9?+

Add every digit of your full date of birth (day, month and year) and keep adding the digits of the total until one digit is left. If the final digit is 9, your Bhagyank is 9. You can also use the free Bhagyank Calculator.

Moolank guide

What is Moolank in numerology?

Moolank, also called the Birth Number or Radical Number, is the single digit derived from the day of your birth. It describes your natural personality, habits, instincts and the way you respond to life.

How to calculate Moolank

Only your birth day is used. If you were born on the 7th, your Moolank is 7. If you were born on the 28th, add 2 + 8 = 10, then 1 + 0 = 1. Your Moolank is therefore 1.

Moolank and Bhagyank

Moolank reflects your inner nature and day-to-day personality. Bhagyank uses your complete date of birth to describe your broader life direction. Reading both together offers a more complete numerology profile.

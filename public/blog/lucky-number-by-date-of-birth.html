Lucky Number by Date of Birth: How to Find Yours

By Ananya Sharma · 26 November 2026

Your lucky number by date of birth is usually your Mulank, the number you get by adding the digits of your birth day. Your Bhagyank, made from your full date of birth, is your second lucky number and matters most for big decisions. Both take less than a minute to work out, and this guide shows you how, with examples.

Which number is your lucky number?

Indian numerology gives you two main numbers from your date of birth. Both can be called lucky, but they do different jobs.

  • Mulank (Moolank) - your birth number. It comes from the day of the month you were born. It shapes your everyday nature, so most numerologists treat it as your main lucky number for daily things.
  • Bhagyank - your destiny number. It comes from the whole date: day, month and year. It points to the bigger direction of your life, so it is used for long-term choices.

So the short answer is: use your Mulank for small, everyday luck and your Bhagyank for big life decisions. When both numbers are the same, that one number counts twice.

How do you find your lucky number by date of birth?

You only need simple addition. Keep adding the digits until you get a single number from 1 to 9.

  1. Mulank: add the digits of your birth day. Born on the 14th? 1 + 4 = 5. Born on the 29th? 2 + 9 = 11, then 1 + 1 = 2.
  2. Bhagyank: add every digit of your full date of birth, then reduce the total. For 14-08-1990: 1+4+0+8+1+9+9+0 = 32, then 3 + 2 = 5.

If you were born on the 1st to the 9th, your Mulank is just that day. Nothing to add.

How can you use your lucky number in daily life?

Here are a few simple ways people use their number:

  • Dates: plan a small but important task on one of your lucky dates.
  • House or flat number: when two homes feel equal, some people pick the one whose number adds up to their Mulank or a friendly number.
  • Phone or vehicle number: if you get to choose, a total that matches your number can feel like a nice bonus.
  • Big decisions: check if the date also suits your Bhagyank. Your two numbers working together is explained in the Mulank and Bhagyank combination chart.

Keep it light, though. A lucky number can help you choose between two good options. It should never push you into a bad one.

Can a lucky number really bring you luck?

No number can promise money, a win or a perfect day. Numerology is a traditional system for reflection, not a proven science. Treat your number as a gentle guide, not a rule.

Wanting a bit of luck on your side is very human, and it isn't only an Indian habit. One YouGov poll of Americans found that 12% always or often carry or wear a lucky charm.

The most useful part of a lucky number is how it makes you feel. Picking a "good" date can give you a calm, confident start. But luck favours the prepared. A lucky date won't replace practice before an interview, and it is a bad reason to buy lottery tickets. Put in the work first, then let your number be the cherry on top.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is my lucky number by date of birth?+

Most people use their Mulank as their main lucky number. It is the sum of the digits of your birth day, reduced to 1-9. For example, someone born on the 23rd has 2 + 3 = 5 as their lucky number.

Is Bhagyank my lucky number?+

Your Bhagyank is your destiny number. It is also seen as lucky, but it is used mainly for big, long-term choices. Your Mulank is the one used for everyday luck.

What if my Mulank and Bhagyank are the same?+

Then that one number is doubly important for you. Its good traits and its weak spots both show up more strongly, and dates that add up to it are seen as extra favourable.

Can my lucky number change?+

No. Your Mulank and Bhagyank come from your date of birth, so they stay the same all your life. What changes is how you use them, for example picking good dates each month.

Which number is the luckiest in numerology?+

No single number is the luckiest for everyone. In Vedic numerology the best number for you is the one that matches your own birth numbers or is friendly to them.

Mulank and Bhagyank Combination: What Your Two Numbers Say Together

By Ananya Sharma · 24 November 2026

Your Mulank shows who you are day to day. Your Bhagyank shows where your life is heading. When the planets behind the two numbers are friends, life tends to feel smoother. When they clash, you feel a pull in two directions. The chart below shows all 81 combinations at a glance.

Step 1: Find your Mulank and Bhagyank

You need just your date of birth.

  • Mulank comes from the day you were born. Born on the 23rd? 2 + 3 = 5, so your Mulank is 5.
  • Bhagyank comes from the full date. For 23-07-1994: 2+3+0+7+1+9+9+4 = 35, then 3 + 5 = 8. Your Bhagyank is 8.

Don't want to add it up by hand? The free Bhagyank Calculator shows both numbers in one click.

How a Mulank and Bhagyank combination works

In Indian numerology, each number from 1 to 9 belongs to a planet: 1 Sun, 2 Moon, 3 Jupiter, 4 Rahu, 5 Mercury, 6 Venus, 7 Ketu, 8 Saturn and 9 Mars.

Classical Vedic astrology says some planets are natural friends and some don't get along. So when you put your Mulank and Bhagyank side by side, you are really asking one question: do these two planets work as a team?

  • Friendly - your nature and your life path pull the same way. Things tend to click.
  • Mixed - one planet likes the other, but the feeling isn't returned. You get some support and some friction.
  • Tricky - the planets don't get along. You may feel torn between what you want and where life pushes you.
  • Same - both numbers are equal, so that planet's traits are doubled, good and bad.

Numerologists don't agree on Ketu (7), so its boxes say "Varies". Some modern numerologists also use their own lists, so you may see small differences on other websites.

What does each number bring to the mix?

Before you read your combination, it helps to know what each number adds on its own. Here is a quick guide. Tap a number to read its full meaning.

  • Number 1 (Sun) brings drive, confidence and a wish to lead.
  • Number 2 (Moon) brings care, feelings and a gift for working with people.
  • Number 3 (Jupiter) brings wisdom, teaching and a hopeful outlook.
  • Number 4 (Rahu) brings hard work and a habit of doing things differently.
  • Number 5 (Mercury) brings a quick mind, good talk and a love of change.
  • Number 6 (Venus) brings love, beauty and a strong pull toward family.
  • Number 7 (Ketu) brings deep thinking and an interest in the spiritual side of life.
  • Number 8 (Saturn) brings patience, discipline and slow but steady success.
  • Number 9 (Mars) brings courage, energy and a fighting spirit.

Your Mulank shows which of these you use every day. Your Bhagyank shows which one your life keeps pulling you toward. When you know both, you can see why some choices feel easy and others take more effort.

Three real examples

  • Mulank 1 + Bhagyank 9 (Friendly): the Sun and Mars are good friends. This person is a natural leader who also has the energy to act. The only watch-out is a hot temper when both kick in at once.
  • Mulank 6 + Bhagyank 1 (Tricky): Venus loves comfort and harmony, while the Sun wants to lead and shine. This person may feel stuck between "keep everyone happy" and "do it my way". Knowing this makes it easier to pick the right moment for each.
  • Mulank 2 + Bhagyank 5 (Mixed): the Moon treats Mercury as a friend, but Mercury doesn't return the favour. Heart and head don't always agree here, so writing things down before a big decision helps.

Is a tricky combination bad?

Not at all. A tricky combination doesn't mean bad luck. It means your two numbers want different things, so you have to steer a bit more on purpose.

Plenty of people with tricky combinations do very well. They often grow more than people whose numbers agree, because they learn to balance two sides of themselves. As the saying goes, smooth seas never made a skilled sailor.

What helps most is simply knowing your pattern. Read the full meaning of your Bhagyank number and your Mulank, then look for the places where they agree. That's where your strengths are.

How much should you rely on it?

Interest in this kind of reading isn't limited to India. A recent Pew Research Center survey found that 3 in 10 U.S. adults consult astrology, tarot cards or fortune tellers. People clearly find value in it.

Still, use your combination as a mirror, not a rulebook. It can help you understand why you react the way you do. Your choices, effort and the people around you shape your life far more than any single chart.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Mulank and Bhagyank combination is best?+

Combinations where the two planets are natural friends are seen as the smoothest, such as 1 with 9, 1 with 3, 3 with 9, 5 with 6, 6 with 8 and 2 with 1. But every combination has strengths, so there is no single best one.

What happens if Mulank and Bhagyank are the same?+

If both numbers are the same, that planet's traits are doubled. You get extra strength in that area, but its weak points can also show up more strongly.

Is Mulank more important than Bhagyank?+

Neither is more important. Mulank describes your everyday nature, and Bhagyank describes the direction of your life. Reading them together gives the clearest picture.

Can a bad combination be fixed?+

Your numbers come from your date of birth, so they don't change. Many people use lucky colours, days or name spelling as traditional remedies, but the most useful step is understanding your pattern and planning around it.

Lo Shu Grid Missing Number 8: Meaning and Simple Remedies

By Ananya Sharma · 28 November 2026

A missing 8 in the Lo Shu Grid is linked with Saturn, so it points to money handling, patience and long-term planning. It does not mean money trouble. It means these habits may need conscious effort. First check that your Mulank or Bhagyank does not already fill the 8, because that happens often.

What does a missing 8 mean in the Lo Shu Grid?

In the Lo Shu Grid, 8 sits in the bottom-left box. Numerologists link it with Saturn (Shani), the planet of hard work, patience and karma. In the grid, 8 stands for how you handle money and how long you can wait for results.

When the 8 box is empty, traditional readings say you may notice some of these patterns:

  • Money comes in but seems to slip away, with little left at the end of the month.
  • You want results fast and lose interest when progress is slow.
  • You start many plans but find it hard to stick to one for years.
  • Paperwork, bills and long-term planning feel boring, so they get pushed aside.

These are signs to watch, not a verdict. Many people with an empty 8 box manage money very well. They simply had to learn it, while others pick it up more easily.

Is 8 really missing from your grid? Check this first

The most common mistake is to look only at the digits of your date of birth. In the Indian method, your Mulank (birth day added up) and your Bhagyank (full date added up) also go into the grid. Quite often one of them brings the 8 back.

Here are four real dates. None of them has the digit 8 written in it:

Date of birthMulankBhagyankIs 8 in the grid?

17-06-19941 + 7 = 837 → 10 → 1Yes - the Mulank fills it
05-12-1970525 → 7No - 8 is really missing
03-04-2001310 → 1No - 8 is really missing

Worked example: 05-12-1970

  1. Digits of the date, without zeros: 5, 1, 2, 1, 9, 7. No 8 here.
  2. Mulank: the birth day is 5, so the Mulank is 5.
  3. Bhagyank: 0 + 5 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 9 + 7 + 0 = 25, and 2 + 5 = 7.
  4. Grid numbers: 1, 1, 2, 5, 5, 7, 7, 9. Missing: 3, 4, 6 and 8.

So before you worry, make your Lo Shu Grid online. It adds the Mulank and Bhagyank for you and shows the real missing numbers. If you are not sure how those two numbers differ, the Moolank Calculator shows your birth number on its own.

How common is a missing 8?

We counted every date from 1 January 1960 to 31 December 2009, which is 18,263 dates, using the same method as above. Here is what we found:

Group of datesShare with no 8 in the grid
All dates, 1960-2009About 49%
Born in the 1960s, 1970s, 1990s or 2000sAbout 61%
Born in the 1980s (1980-1989)0% - the year always has an 8

So outside the 1980s, about 6 in 10 people have an empty 8 box. Something that common is not a curse. It is just one part of your chart that asks for a little extra effort.

Which planes and yogs does a missing 8 break?

Number 8 is part of three lines in the grid. If 8 is missing, none of these three can be complete:

Plane or yogNumbersWhat it shows
Practical plane8 - 1 - 6Hands-on work and getting things done
Thought plane4 - 3 - 8Ideas and how you plan them
Silver Yog2 - 5 - 8Property and steady wealth

Look at the 05-12-1970 example again. It has 2 and 5, so the Silver Yog is just one number short. People in this spot are often told that steady saving matters more for them than for most.

The other five lines do not use 8 at all, so a missing 8 leaves them alone. If your grid has the 9-5-1 Will plane, for example, that strength stays. You can see all eight planes explained on the Lo Shu Grid Calculator page.

Does a missing 8 mean money problems?

No. It does not mean you will be poor or in debt. In numerology it only points to an area where good habits may not come on their own.

And this is a skill most people have to learn anyway. The S&P financial literacy survey, which interviewed more than 1.5 lakh adults in over 140 countries, found that 76% of Indian adults did not properly understand key money ideas. Missing 8 or not, almost everyone gains from learning the basics.

Lo Shu Grid missing 8 remedies that actually help

The best remedies build what Saturn stands for: patience, order and steady effort with money. Start with daily habits:

  • Pay yourself first: on salary day, move a fixed amount into savings before you spend anything.
  • Track one month: write down every expense for 30 days. Most people find one or two leaks right away.
  • Use a 48-hour rule: for any big purchase, wait two days before you buy.
  • Pick one long goal: a course, a fund or a skill, and give it a small slot every week.
  • Keep papers in one place: bills, policies and bank papers in one file, checked once a month.

As the old saying goes, a penny saved is a penny earned. Small amounts saved every month add up faster than one big effort once a year. And Rome wasn't built in a day, so judge your progress over months, not days.

Now the traditional remedies, which people follow as per their own belief:

  • Saturday is Saturn's day. Some people choose it to start a savings plan or give to those in need.
  • Dark blue, black and grey are seen as Saturn's colours. A wallet or diary in these colours is a simple reminder.
  • Showing respect to elders and workers, and being on time, are classic ways to "please" Saturn in Indian tradition.
  • Blue Sapphire (Neelam) is the stone of 8, but it is known as a strong stone. Read the gemstone guide by birth date and talk to an expert before wearing it.

To learn more about number 8 and Saturn, see Bhagyank 8 and Saturn.

What should you avoid with a missing 8?

  • Don't panic-buy remedies: no stone, yantra or ritual can promise wealth.
  • Don't skip the check: many people "fix" an 8 that their Bhagyank already filled.
  • Don't blame the grid: treat it as a nudge to build habits, not an excuse.
  • Don't read one box alone: look at your full grid along with your lucky number by date of birth, since strong numbers elsewhere can balance an empty box.

Your grid is a map of tendencies. Your daily choices are what decide where you end up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean if 8 is missing in my Lo Shu Grid?+

Number 8 is ruled by Saturn and stands for money handling and patience. If it is missing, traditional readings say saving money and waiting for results may not come naturally, so you may need to build those habits on purpose.

Can my Bhagyank fill the missing 8?+

Yes. In the Indian method your Mulank and Bhagyank are added to the grid. If either of them is 8, the 8 box is filled. For example, 12-03-1991 has no 8 in the date, but its Bhagyank is 8.

Why does nobody born in the 1980s have a missing 8?+

Every year from 1980 to 1989 has the digit 8 in it, so the 8 box is always filled for people born in that decade.

Which planes need number 8?+

Three lines use 8: the Practical plane (8-1-6), the Thought plane (4-3-8) and the Silver Yog (2-5-8). A missing 8 keeps all three incomplete.

Should I wear Blue Sapphire for a missing 8?+

Blue Sapphire (Neelam) is the traditional stone of 8, but it is considered a strong stone. Many people start with habits and traditional practices instead. Always consult a qualified expert before wearing it.

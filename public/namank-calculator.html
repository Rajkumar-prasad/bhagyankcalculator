Namank numerology guide

What is Namank?

Namank, also called Name Number, is the single digit calculated from the letters in your name. In traditional numerology, your name is connected with your public identity, communication style and the way others experience your energy. This free Namank Calculator converts each English letter into a number and reduces the total to 1-9.

How to calculate Namank

Write your commonly used full name, assign a value to each letter using the Chaldean-style chart, add the values and reduce the total to a single digit. Use the result as a reflective numerology insight rather than a fixed prediction.

Namank, Moolank and Bhagyank

Namank is based on your name, while Moolank comes from your birth day and Bhagyank uses your complete date of birth. For a broader reading, try our Moolank Calculator and Bhagyank Calculator, then explore the free gemstone recommendation guide.

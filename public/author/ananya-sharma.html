About Ananya

Ananya Sharma (अनन्या शर्मा) is the pen name behind the numerology guides on Bhagyank Calculator. She writes about Bhagyank (destiny number), Mulank (birth number) and Namank (name number), and explains what each number from 1 to 9 traditionally represents in Vedic numerology - its ruling planet, nature, lucky colors, lucky days and gemstone.

Her guides show the full calculation step by step, with worked date-of-birth examples, so you can check every result yourself instead of taking a number on trust. She writes in both English and Hindi, so the same method is easy to follow in either language.

How she writes the guides

Every guide follows the traditional Vedic numerology method: add every digit of the date of birth and reduce the total to a single number from 1 to 9. Each guide shows the full working with real date-of-birth examples, so you can check the result yourself. The guides explain what each number traditionally represents and do not make promises about your future.

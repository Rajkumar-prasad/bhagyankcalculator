What is a Lo Shu Grid?

A Lo Shu Grid is a 3 x 3 box of numbers from 1 to 9. In numerology, you fill it with the digits of your date of birth to see which numbers you have plenty of, and which ones are missing.

The grid comes from an old Chinese story. A turtle came out of the Luo River with a pattern of dots on its shell. The dots made a "magic square" where every row, column and diagonal adds up to 15. Today the grid is widely used in Indian numerology, often next to your Mulank and Bhagyank.

How to Make Your Lo Shu Grid (Example)

Let's use a real date: 15 August 1990.

  1. Write the date as digits: 1, 5, 0, 8, 1, 9, 9, 0
  2. Drop the zeros: 1, 5, 8, 1, 9, 9
  3. Add the Mulank (birth day 15 → 1 + 5 = 6)
  4. Add the Bhagyank (1+5+0+8+1+9+9+0 = 33 → 3 + 3 = 6)
  5. Put each digit in its box. Repeated digits share a box.

How to Read Your Lo Shu Grid

Look at three things:

  • Present numbers - qualities that come easily to you.
  • Repeated numbers - extra strength in that area. Too many repeats (four or more) can tip into overdoing it.
  • Missing numbers - qualities you may need to build on purpose.

Then check the planes below. A plane is "complete" when all three of its numbers are in your grid.

Missing Numbers: Should You Worry?

No. Almost every date of birth leaves a few boxes empty, because a date only has eight digits and many of them repeat. So don't make a mountain out of a molehill if your grid looks gappy.

A missing number simply shows where you may need a little more effort. For example, a missing 4 can mean you find routines boring, so a simple daily to-do list may help you more than it helps others.

How Much Should You Rely on It?

Number-based readings are popular for a reason. In a Pew Research Center survey of 29,999 Indian adults, 49% of Hindus said they believe in astrology, and numerology tools like the Lo Shu Grid come from that same family of traditions. Use your grid as a mirror for self-reflection, not as a final verdict on your life.

Lo Shu Grid vs Bhagyank

Your Bhagyank is a single number that shows the overall direction of your life. The Lo Shu Grid breaks your date into pieces and shows the mix of strengths behind that number. Start with your Bhagyank, then use the grid to see the details. You can also read the meaning of each Bhagyank from 1 to 9.

Lo Shu Grid: Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a Lo Shu Grid from date of birth?+

Write your full date of birth as digits and drop the zeros. Place each digit in its fixed box on the 4-9-2, 3-5-7, 8-1-6 grid. Most Indian numerologists also add your Mulank (birth day reduced to one digit) and your Bhagyank (full date reduced to one digit). Repeated digits go in the same box.

What does a missing number in the Lo Shu Grid mean?+

A missing number points to a quality that does not come naturally to you, so you may need to work on it. It is not a bad sign. Almost every date of birth has some missing numbers.

Why are zeros not used in the Lo Shu Grid?+

The grid only has boxes for the numbers 1 to 9, so there is no place for 0. That is why a date like 10-10-2000 leaves many boxes empty.

Is the Lo Shu Grid the same as Bhagyank?+

No. Your Bhagyank is one number that comes from adding your whole date of birth. The Lo Shu Grid looks at every digit separately and shows which numbers are present, repeated or missing. Together they give a fuller reading.

Does Kua number go in the Lo Shu Grid?+

Some numerologists also add the Kua number, which depends on your birth year and gender. This calculator uses the more common method with your birth date digits, Mulank and Bhagyank.
